Tax returns made simple

We prepare and file your personal tax return for a flat fee of £185, VAT included. Everything from start to finish, with no hidden costs.

What we do

We take your information, prepare your personal tax return, and file it with HMRC. You do not need to understand the form, work out which boxes apply, or spend an evening second-guessing whether you have claimed the right things.

You submit your details through a short online sign-up. An accountant reviews them, comes back to you with any questions, and prepares the return. Once you confirm you are happy, we submit it to HMRC.

What is included

  • Preparation — your return completed by an accountant, not by software you have to drive yourself.
  • Unlimited questions — ask as much as you like before you pay. There is no charge for understanding your own tax position.
  • Filing with HMRC — we submit the return and confirm when it has been accepted.
  • One flat fee — £185 including VAT, however complicated your affairs turn out to be.

Why the flat fee matters

Most accountants charge by the hour, which means every question has a price attached and you find out what the return cost once it is already done. It also means people avoid asking things they should ask.

We charge one fee, agreed before we start. If your return takes longer than expected, that is our problem, not an invoice.

Who this is for

  • First-time filers — you have been told you need to file and are not sure where to begin.
  • Landlords — rental income to declare, and expenses you may be able to offset.
  • Investors — dividends, savings interest or capital gains to report.
  • CIS subcontractors — gross or net payments from construction work.
  • Sole traders — self-employed income and allowable expenses.

Late filing costs more than filing

HMRC charges an automatic £100 for a late return even when no tax is owed, with further penalties after three months. If a deadline has passed, tell us — it is better to file late than not at all.

Not sure whether you need to file?

You may not. If all your income is taxed through PAYE and you have nothing else to declare, there is often nothing to do. Ask us and we will tell you honestly rather than sell you a service you do not need.
