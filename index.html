HMRC's penalties are automatic

A late return costs £100 even if you owe no tax at all, with further daily penalties after three months and interest on unpaid tax from the day it was due.

If a deadline has already passed, do not keep waiting. Tell us and we will help you sort it — filing late is always better than not filing.
